Teen2Teen 2
Topic-Based Speaking Questions
Units 1–12  •  120 Questions
Simple speaking questions for elementary students • Practice present tenses, questions, and everyday vocabulary
Unit 1: We're wearing our new uniform!
Topic: 👕 New Uniforms & Family
1. What are you wearing right now?
2. Are you wearing your school uniform today?
3. What color is your new uniform?
4. Who is your brother or sister?
5. What is your mom doing right now?
6. Are your friends wearing uniforms?
7. Do you like wearing a uniform? Why or why not?
8. What are you doing after school?
9. Is your dad at home now?
10. Tell me about your family. Who lives with you?

Unit 2: Are you doing your homework?
Topic: 📚 Homework & Activities at Home
1. Are you doing your homework right now?
2. What are you doing after school today?
3. Where do you usually do your homework?
4. Do you like doing homework? Why?
5. Are your parents helping you with homework?
6. What subject are you studying today?
7. Is it easy or hard to do homework at home?
8. What are your friends doing now?
9. Do you watch TV after you finish homework?
10. Who checks your homework at home?

Unit 3: What are you doing this year?
Topic: 🏫 This School Year & Activities
1. What are you doing in school this year?
2. Are you playing any sports this year?
3. What is your favorite subject this year?
4. Who is your best friend this year?
5. Are you learning English this year?
6. What club or activity are you in?
7. Do you have a new teacher this year?
8. What are you good at this year?
9. Are you singing or dancing in school?
10. Tell us about your best friend.

6. How many students are in your class?
7. Do you eat eggs for breakfast?
8. What is your favorite fruit?
9. Are there any cars in front of your house?

Unit 7: How do you get there?
Topic: 🚌 Getting Around & Transportation
1. How do you get to school every day?
2. Do you walk to school or take the bus?
3. What time do you leave home for school?
4. Is your school far from your home?
5. How do your parents go to work?
6. Do you ride a bicycle?
7. Where is the nearest park from your house?
8. What time does your school start?
9. Do you take the bus or subway?
10. Describe the way from your home to school.

Unit 8: What do your parents do?
Topic: 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Parents' Jobs & Work

4. Where does your dad work?

Unit 9: Feel better!
Topic: 🤒 Health, Feeling Sick & Getting Better
1. How do you feel today?
2. Are you feeling sick or tired?
3. What do you do when you have a cold?
4. Do you stay home from school when you are sick?
5. Who takes care of you when you are not well?
6. Do you eat healthy food every day?
7. How many hours do you sleep at night?
8. Do you exercise or play sports to stay healthy?
9. What makes you feel better when you are sad?
10. Are you happy today? Why?

Unit 12: She's smarter than me!
Topic: 📊 Comparing People & Family

6. Is your school bigger than your house?
