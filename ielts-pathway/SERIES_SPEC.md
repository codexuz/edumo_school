# IELTS Pathway A1–B2: series specification

This file replaces the four "English Pathway" guides (A1, A2, B1 and B2). It is the source of truth for the whole series. The course map for each level is in `levels/`.

## 1. What the series is

**IELTS Pathway** is a four-level course (A1, A2, B1, B2). It takes a learner from beginner to IELTS readiness. Each level has two books:

| Book | Purpose | Size per unit |
|---|---|---|
| **Student's Book (SB)** | Teaches the language and skills. Every lesson ends in an IELTS-format task at the right difficulty for the level. | 8–10 pages |
| **Workbook (WB)** | More practice of the same unit, for homework or self-study, with new texts and recordings. | 6–8 pages |

Every level has 12 units, 3 Progress Tests and 1 Final Test.

### How this differs from the General English guides

| General English guides said | IELTS Pathway does |
|---|---|
| "Not an IELTS book"; IELTS formats should not dominate | IELTS formats are introduced from A1 at the right difficulty for each level and are the endpoint of every skills lesson. |
| Everyday English pages, final projects, presentations, long starter units | Removed. Every page practises language that is tested in IELTS or needed to reach it. |
| Student's Book in English only; Uzbek only in a separate guide | Uzbek is used where it saves time: vocabulary meanings, short grammar notes, and common Uzbek-speaker errors. Instructions stay in English. |
| Writing equal in size to every other strand | Grammar, Vocabulary, Listening, Reading and Speaking are the core strands. Writing is shorter at A1–A2 and grows into full IELTS Task 1 and Task 2 at B1–B2. |
| Separate teacher's guide and LMS schema as early deliverables | Deferred. Content is stored as JSON, which can later feed an LMS. Teacher notes come after the books. |

**Kept from the guides:** the CEFR grammar order, recycling vocabulary, original texts and recordings, answer keys checked against the final version, transcripts at the back, stable IDs, and a pilot unit before mass production.

## 2. Target learners and honest outcomes

The books are for teen and adult learners in Uzbekistan who want an IELTS score and need to build their English from the level they have now.

| Level | Entry | After the level (typical, not guaranteed) | IELTS link |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Beginner | Can complete simple forms and answer basic personal questions | Foundation: IELTS formats, spelling and numbers, Speaking Part 1 |
| A2 | A1 | Can handle familiar topics and short texts | About band 3.5–4.0 readiness |
| B1 | A2 | Can follow and give opinions; writes organised paragraphs | About band 4.5–5.5 |
| B2 | B1 | Can handle full IELTS tasks with control | About band 5.5–6.5 |

Bands are estimates for planning. The books never promise a band score.

## 3. The exam-skill ladder

Each IELTS task type is introduced at the level where learners can do it, then made longer and harder. **Bold** marks the level where a task type is first introduced.

### Listening
| | A1 | A2 | B1 | B2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recordings | Section 1-style dialogues, 1–2 min, slow and clear | Sections 1–2, 2–3 min | Sections 1–3, 3–4 min | Sections 1–4, full length and speed |
| Task types | **Form / note completion** (names, spelling, numbers), **MCQ (A/B/C)**, **simple map labelling** | **Table completion**, **matching**, **flow-chart** | **Sentence completion**, **plan / diagram labelling**, MCQ with 2 answers | All types at test level; **summary completion** |
| Skills | Letters, numbers, dates, prices; corrections ("No, sorry, it's…") | Predicting the answer type; distractors | Paraphrase, signposting, speaker attitude | Following a lecture; note-taking at speed |

### Reading
| | A1 | A2 | B1 | B2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Text length | 120–250 words (notices, profiles, emails, short articles) | 250–400 words | 450–700 words | 750–900 words, Academic style |
| Task types | **True / False**, **short answers** and **sentence completion** (ONE WORD / A NUMBER), **matching** | **TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN**, **matching headings**, **summary with word box**, MCQ | **YES / NO / NOT GIVEN**, **matching information / features**, **diagram labelling** | All 14 Academic task types at test difficulty |
| Skills | Scanning for names, numbers and key words | Skimming for the main idea; synonyms | Paraphrase; the writer's view | Time management: 3 passages in 60 minutes |

### Speaking
| | A1 | A2 | B1 | B2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Parts | **Part 1** (short answers, "+1" detail) | Part 1 extended; **Part 2** (1 min, with notes); **Part 3** (simple *why* questions) | Parts 1–3 full; Part 2 for 1.5–2 min | Full test with band-descriptor self-check |
| Focus | Personal information, contractions, clear numbers and letters | Reasons with *because*; past experiences | Opinions, comparisons, speculation | Fluency, range, precision, extending answers |

### Writing
| | A1 | A2 | B1 | B2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Task 1 | Forms; describing a picture or table in simple sentences | **Simple bar chart / table** (higher, lower, most); **GT informal letter** | Line, bar, pie, table, **process**, **map** (150 words) | All types at test level, with a clear overview |
| Task 2 | Sentences with *and / but / because* | **Opinion paragraph** (topic sentence + reason + example) | **Essays**: opinion, discussion, advantages/disadvantages, problem/solution (200–250 words) | Full 250+ word essays, all question types |

## 4. Unit blueprint: Student's Book

The order is always the same, so learners know where they are. Page counts are guides, not quotas.

| # | Section | Contents | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Opener** | Title; "Exam focus" box (which IELTS tasks the unit trains); 4–5 "I can…" goals; Speaking Part 1 warm-up questions | ½–1 |
| 2 | **Vocabulary** | Topic word set with Uzbek meaning, part of speech and an example; collocations; word families from B1. Exercises move from controlled practice to use. | 1½–2 |
| 3 | **Grammar** | Form table; meaning; 4–6 examples; **Uzbek-speaker errors** box; 3–4 exercises ending in an exam-style task | 2 |
| 4 | **Listening** | Pre-listening prediction; an **Exam skill** box; one IELTS-format task (plus one detail task at A1–A2) | 1–1½ |
| 5 | **Reading** | An original text; an **Exam skill** box; 2 IELTS-format tasks | 1½–2 |
| 6 | **Speaking** | The IELTS part(s) for the level; model answers; useful language; a pronunciation point | 1 |
| 7 | **Writing** | A model; one structure or accuracy point; a task with a word range; a checklist | 1 |
| 8 | **Exam practice** | A mini-test in IELTS formats: vocabulary, grammar, listening or reading; a score box; "I can" check | 1 |

**Back of the book:** grammar reference, an A–Z word list with Uzbek meanings, audioscripts, and the answer key.

## 5. Unit blueprint: Workbook

The workbook follows the Student's Book unit section by section, with **new** material (not the same exercises again):

1. **Vocabulary+**: spelling, collocations, word formation, Uzbek→English translation, gap-fill in a short text.
2. **Grammar+**: 3–5 drills from controlled to free, error correction (Uzbek-speaker errors), translation sentences.
3. **Listening+**: a new recording in the unit's exam format.
4. **Reading+**: a new text in the unit's exam format.
5. **Speaking+**: a question bank for the unit's IELTS part(s), answer frames, and a self-recording checklist.
6. **Writing+**: information transfer or a guided task, then an independent task.
7. **Self-check**: score per section and "I can" statements.

The Workbook has its own answer key and audioscripts at the back.

## 6. Language strands

### Grammar
* Follow the CEFR order in the level course maps. There is one main grammar point per unit (two only if they are closely linked).
* Every grammar point has: form (positive, negative, question), meaning, examples, Uzbek-speaker errors, controlled practice, and **an exam application**. For example: Part 1 answers with *be* (A1); describing a chart with comparatives (A2); passive verbs for a process (B1).
* Grammar is recycled in later units' texts and tasks.

### Vocabulary
* 20–25 items per unit at A1–A2, 25–35 at B1–B2, chosen from IELTS topic areas and by how often they are used.
* Every item has: word, part of speech, Uzbek meaning, example sentence. IPA and word families are added from B1. At A1–A2 the focus is on spelling, because IELTS answers must be spelled correctly.
* Every unit also builds an **IELTS core**: numbers, dates and spelling (A1–A2); paraphrase and synonyms (A2+); chart language (A2+); linking words (B1+); academic word families (B2).
* Recycling: each unit's texts reuse vocabulary from earlier units; Progress Tests test vocabulary from earlier units too.

## 7. Texts, recordings and originality

* All reading texts, recordings, questions and model answers are **original**. Nothing is copied from published IELTS books, Cambridge material, or the websites used for the KET/PET books.
* Facts must be checked or kept general. Names and settings are international, with Uzbekistan included where it fits.
* Every recording has: an ID (`A1_U01_L01`), speaker labels, notes for the recording session, and the answer in the script. The QR code beside each task is added after the audio is recorded and hosted.
* Every answer must be supported by the text or recording, and every distractor must be clearly wrong.

## 8. Tests

| Test | When | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Exam practice | End of every unit | 1 page, about 15 items, mixed IELTS formats |
| Progress Test 1–3 | After Units 3, 6 and 9 | Half an IELTS test at the right difficulty: Listening, Reading, Speaking cards, a short Writing task, and a Grammar/Vocabulary section |
| Final Test | After Unit 12 | A full test at the right difficulty for the level (at B2, a full IELTS-format mock) |

Scores are reported per skill. At B1–B2, Writing and Speaking self-checks use simplified IELTS band descriptors, labelled "practice estimate".

## 9. IDs and files

```
ielts-pathway/
  SERIES_SPEC.md            this file
  levels/a1.md … b2.md      course maps (authoritative unit plans)
  TRACKER.md                production status
  OPEN_QUESTIONS.md         decisions needing approval
  scripts/                  build, render and validation (shared by all levels)
  a1/ a2/ b1/ b2/
    data/sb-unit-01.json    Student's Book unit
    data/wb-unit-01.json    Workbook unit
    dist/                   PDFs
  assets/photos/            Unsplash photos + credits.json
```

* Exercise IDs: `A1_U01_SB_VOC_01`, `A1_U01_WB_GR_03` (level, unit, book, section, number). Audio IDs: `A1_U01_L01` (SB) and `A1_U01_WL01` (WB).
* IDs are never reused for a different activity.
* `scripts/validate.py` checks each unit file. It reports duplicate IDs, closed exercises without answers, listening tasks without a script, missing Uzbek meanings, and reading texts outside the level's word range.

## 10. Design

* The design matches the house style of the repo's other books: A4, two columns, the Impulse brand, Unbounded and Open Sans fonts.
* **Photo-led pages.** Each unit opens with a full-width photo carrying the unit number and title. The Listening, Reading, Speaking and Writing sections get a photo each, and the Workbook gets one in its Reading section. Photos come from Unsplash through `scripts/unsplash.py`: the script registers every download with Unsplash, as its API rules require, and stores the photographer's name for the credit line under each photo and the credits list at the back.
* **Every section starts with a "Goal" strip** (for example "Goal: complete a form while you listen"), so learners always know what the section is for.
* Each skill has its own colour, so pages are easy to scan: Vocabulary teal, Grammar indigo, Listening blue, Reading navy, Speaking green, Writing amber, Exam practice red.
* Instructions are bold and numbered. "Exam skill", "Uzbek-speaker errors" and "Tip" use fixed box styles. Pages must still read clearly when printed in greyscale.
* There is enough writing space for gap-fills and writing tasks. Exercises are never split across columns.

## 11. Production workflow

1. **Foundation (done):** this spec, the four course maps, the tracker and open questions.
2. **Pilot:** A1 Unit 1 (SB + WB), validated and rendered. **The owner reviews it before anything else is produced.**
3. **A1 production:** two units per batch. After each batch: validate, check answer keys against texts and scripts, update the tracker.
4. **A1 tests and reference:** Progress Tests, Final Test, grammar reference, word list, scripts, answer key, covers.
5. **Audio:** record the scripts, then add QR codes and test them.
6. **A2, B1 and B2** in the same way. Each level starts with its own pilot unit.

## 12. Quality checklist (every unit)

* [ ] Topic, grammar and exam tasks match the level course map.
* [ ] Every section ends in an IELTS-format task at the right difficulty for the level.
* [ ] Vocabulary items appear in the unit's texts, recordings or exercises; Uzbek meanings are checked.
* [ ] Grammar explanation, tables and examples agree; the errors box is accurate.
* [ ] Every closed exercise has one defensible answer; the key matches the final text.
* [ ] Every listening answer is spoken in the script, including corrections and distractors.
* [ ] Reading text length is within the level range; every answer can be found in the text.
* [ ] Speaking models are natural and short enough to imitate.
* [ ] No filler pages; nothing copied; no unchecked facts.
* [ ] `validate.py` passes; the PDF has no clipped content or split exercises.
