# IELTS Pathway A1: course map

**Aim:** learners build the basics of English and get used to the easiest IELTS formats: forms with names and numbers, short texts with True/False and one-word answers, and Speaking Part 1 about themselves.
**Grammar order:** be → possessives / have got → present simple → there is/are → countable / some / any → can, like + -ing → wh- questions → imperatives → present continuous → going to → comparatives → was/were and the past simple (regular verbs).

| Unit | Topic | Grammar | Vocabulary | Listening (IELTS task) | Reading (IELTS task) | Speaking | Writing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **About Me** | be: am / is / are (+ − ?), subject pronouns, a/an + job | alphabet and spelling, numbers 0–100, phone numbers, countries and nationalities, personal information | Form completion: enrolment form (spelling, numbers, a correction) | Profiles: True/False; sentence completion (ONE WORD / A NUMBER) | Part 1: name, home town, work or study; the "+1" rule; contractions | Complete a form; 5–6 sentence profile; capital letters |
| 2 | **Family and Friends** | possessive adjectives, possessive 's, have got | family, appearance, personality (friendly, kind, quiet…), ages | Note completion: a family photo (names, ages, jobs) | Matching people to descriptions | Part 1: family, best friend | Describe a family member |
| 3 | **Daily Life** | present simple + − ?, 3rd person -s, adverbs of frequency | routine verbs, time, days, parts of the day | Timetable completion (times, days) | Sentence completion; short answers | Part 1: daily routine, weekends | My typical day (and, but, then) |
| 4 | **Home** | there is / there are, prepositions of place | rooms, furniture, home adjectives | Simple plan labelling (a flat, A–F) | Matching adverts to people | Part 1: your home, your room | Describe your home |
| 5 | **Food and Drink** | countable / uncountable, some / any, how much / how many | food, drinks, meals, containers, prices | Note completion: café order (prices, quantities) | Menu / notice: short answers | Part 1: food you like, meals | Short email inviting a friend to eat |
| 6 | **Free Time** | like / love / hate + -ing, can / can't, object pronouns | hobbies, sports, music, films | MCQ (A/B/C): planning the weekend | True/False (180 words) | Part 1: hobbies, sport | Message: an invitation and a reply |
| 7 | **Work and Study** | wh- questions, prepositions of time (at / on / in) | jobs, workplaces, school subjects | Form completion: job-centre enquiry | Matching headings to short paragraphs (intro) | Part 1: work or studies | Describe a job (who, where, when) |
| 8 | **Town and Transport** | imperatives, prepositions of movement, can for requests | places in town, transport, directions | Map labelling: directions in town | Timetable / notice: short answers | Part 1: your neighbourhood, transport | Give directions in writing |
| 9 | **Shopping and Clothes** | present continuous; this / that / these / those | clothes, colours, sizes, money | MCQ and form: ordering online | Product descriptions: matching | Part 1: shopping, clothes | Describe a picture (seed for Task 1) |
| 10 | **Weather and Seasons** | be going to; and / but / because | weather, months, dates, temperatures | Table completion: a weather forecast | Sentence completion | Part 1: weather, seasons; first "talk for 30 seconds" | Paragraph: my favourite season (because) |
| 11 | **Places and Countries** | comparatives (-er, more), be questions review | geography, adjectives for places | Matching countries to facts | True/False (200 words); table completion | Compare two places | Read a table and write comparison sentences (Task 1 seed) |
| 12 | **Holidays and Travel** | was / were; past simple (common regular verbs) | travel, hotels, holiday activities, dates | Form completion: hotel booking | Holiday email: short answers | Part 1: holidays; 1-minute talk with prompts (Part 2 seed) | Holiday email or postcard |

## Tests
* **Progress Test 1** (Units 1–3), **2** (Units 4–6), **3** (Units 7–9): Listening (form + MCQ, 10 items), Reading (2 short texts, 10 items), Grammar and Vocabulary (20 items), Speaking Part 1 cards, Writing (form + 50 words).
* **Final Test** (Units 1–12): the same format, twice as long, with Writing of 60–80 words.

## Level targets
* Reading texts 120–250 words; recordings 1–2 minutes; writing 30–80 words; Speaking answers of 1–3 sentences.
* About 260 vocabulary items in total (20–25 per unit), each with Uzbek meaning.
