# IELTS Pathway B1: course map

**Aim:** learners work with every IELTS task type and write full Task 1 reports and Task 2 essays (shorter and with more support than in the real test). Speaking covers all three parts.
**Grammar order:** present perfect vs past simple, present perfect continuous → relative clauses, indirect questions → first and second conditionals, may / might → future forms, time clauses → passive (present / past) → reported speech, modals of obligation → gerunds and infinitives → modals of deduction, contrast linkers → past perfect, used to → passive extension, non-defining relative clauses → narrative tenses, used to / would → second conditional review, quantifiers.

| Unit | Topic | Grammar | Vocabulary | Listening (IELTS task) | Reading (IELTS task) | Speaking | Writing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Tests
* Progress Tests 1–3: Listening (2 sections, 20 items), Reading (1 passage, 13 items), Writing (one Task 1 or Task 2), Speaking (all parts), Grammar and Vocabulary (20).
* Final Test: Listening 4 sections (40), Reading 2 passages (26), Writing Task 1 + Task 2, Speaking test, with simplified band-descriptor self-check.

## Level targets
* Reading 450–700 words; recordings 3–4 minutes; Task 1 at 120–150 words, Task 2 at 200–250 words; Part 2 talks of 1.5–2 minutes.
* About 360 vocabulary items with IPA, word families and Uzbek meanings; a linking-words bank.
