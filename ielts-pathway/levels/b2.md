# IELTS Pathway B2: course map

**Aim:** full IELTS performance. Learners work with Academic-length passages, all four Listening sections at test speed, full Task 1 and Task 2 writing, and the complete Speaking test with band-descriptor feedback. Grammar and vocabulary focus on range and precision, which raises Writing and Speaking bands.
**Grammar order:** tense and aspect review → modals of deduction (past) → third and mixed conditionals → future continuous / perfect → passive and reporting passive → reporting verbs → gerund / infinitive meaning changes → articles, quantifiers, noun phrases → relative and participle clauses → cleft sentences and inversion → narrative forms → hedging and concession.

## Tests
* Progress Tests 1–3: half IELTS tests (Listening 20, Reading 1 passage, one Writing task, Speaking) with band estimates.
* Final Test: a full IELTS-format mock test (Listening 40, Reading 3 passages / 40, Writing Task 1 + 2, Speaking) with a band conversion table and band-descriptor self-check.

## Level targets
* Reading 750–900 words; recordings at real test length and speed; Task 1 at 150+ words, Task 2 at 250+ words.
* About 400 vocabulary items with IPA, collocations, word families and register notes; Academic Word List strand.
