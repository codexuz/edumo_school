# IELTS Pathway A2: course map

**Aim:** learners talk and write about familiar topics, the past and plans, and meet most IELTS task types in a simplified form. TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN, matching headings, Speaking Part 2 and a first bar chart are all introduced here.
**Grammar order:** past simple → present simple vs continuous → modals of advice and obligation → present perfect (experience) → comparatives and superlatives, too / enough → will / going to and the first conditional → quantifiers and sequencers → can / could and verb patterns → indefinite pronouns and polite requests → past continuous → future arrangements and time clauses → relative clauses (who / which / that) and although.

| Unit | Topic | Grammar | Vocabulary | Listening (IELTS task) | Reading (IELTS task) | Speaking | Writing |

## Tests
* Progress Tests 1–3: Listening (Sections 1–2 style, 20 items), Reading (2 texts, 20 items), Grammar and Vocabulary (20), Speaking Parts 1–2 cards, Writing (Task 1 chart 80 words + 100-word letter or paragraph).
* Final Test: Sections 1–2 in full, 3 reading texts, Writing Task 1 (100 words) and Task 2 (120 words), Speaking Parts 1–3.

## Level targets
* Reading 250–400 words; recordings 2–3 minutes; writing 80–150 words; Part 2 talks of 1 minute.
* About 320 vocabulary items in total (25–30 per unit), with Uzbek meanings, plus a chart-language bank.
